# 🎮 Gaming Lifestyle, Mental Health & Addiction Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Gaming Lifestyle, Mental Health & Addiction Analysis** dashboard is an interactive Power BI project that explores the relationship between gaming behavior, mental health, addiction levels, sleep patterns, productivity, and lifestyle factors. The dashboard transforms raw gaming data into meaningful business insights using interactive visualizations, KPIs, and DAX measures.

This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence can be used to identify behavioral patterns, evaluate mental health risks, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

- Analyze the impact of gaming intensity on mental health.
- Identify relationships between gaming habits, addiction, anxiety, stress, and depression.
- Compare productivity and sleep quality across different gamer generations.
- Evaluate lifestyle factors affecting mental well-being.
- Provide actionable insights using interactive Power BI dashboards.

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 📄 Page 1 – Executive Overview

### KPIs

- Total Gamers
- Average Gaming Hours
- Average Sleep Hours
- Average Mental Health Risk
- Gaming Lifestyle Wellness Score

### Visualizations

- Mental Health Status Distribution
- Gaming Intensity Distribution (Treemap)
- Productivity by Gamer Generation
- Gaming Intensity vs Average Addiction Level
- Wellness Score Gauge

### Key Insights

- Moderate Gamers represent the largest gamer population.
- Moderate Mental Health Risk accounts for the majority of gamers.
- Heavy Gamers exhibit the highest addiction levels.
- Adult Gamers show the highest productivity.
- The overall wellness score indicates opportunities to improve gaming-life balance.

## 📄 Page 2 – Behavioral & Mental Health Analysis

### Visualizations

- Mental Health Risk Matrix
- Gaming Hours vs Anxiety & Addiction Analysis
- Interactive Decomposition Tree
- Behavioral Factor Analysis
- Interactive Slicers

### Key Insights

- Gaming intensity has a stronger impact on addiction than anxiety.
- Heavy Gamers consistently record higher mental health risk.
- Sleep quality significantly influences mental health outcomes.
- Senior and Adult Gamers generally maintain healthier sleep patterns.
- Interactive decomposition analysis identifies key behavioral drivers of mental health risk.

## 📄 Page 3 – Lifestyle & Psychological Insights

### Visualizations

- Gaming Behavior Across Age Groups
- Psychological Health Profile
- Productivity Funnel
- Sleep Pattern Ribbon Chart
- BMI Category Distribution

### Key Insights

- High-risk gamers show higher anxiety, stress, depression, and addiction scores.
- Moderate Productivity is the most common productivity level.
